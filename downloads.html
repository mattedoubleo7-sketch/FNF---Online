Update log - 2026.05.31

Universal Hold Notes

Hold notes now work consistently across the full setlist: sustained notes keep the receptor lit, keep the singer in the held pose, score sustain ticks, and still use each song's existing hold-note skin.

Latest
Universal hold-note behavior

Sustains now run through one shared hold system for every song. One Hit and Shimmy keep the old Challenge Edd hold-note skin, Perseverance keeps its default/pixel sustain skins, and each chart keeps its own visuals.

Previous
Silly Billy retarget

Test of Time chart + Inst.ogg now run inside the Silly Billy slot against Silly Billy's own vocals. Lyric timing, shrink/unshrink, mirror break, and the SO_STAY cutscene were all re-anchored to the new chart.

New Song
Shimmy imported

Wii Funkin's Shimmy is now in the setlist — original hard chart, real split instrumental and vocals, Combat's 3D stage, modchart shader pulses, and the "Not bad, kid" dialogue bloom at the end. BPM 167.

Pixel Area
Pixel BF + camera push

The pixel BF sprite was added to the pixel phase with proper alt-anim support. The camera now zooms in on the duo (background scales with them, no more per-note position jump).

Opponent
spikes-bf opponent

The shrunken phase opponent is now a darkened spikes-bf. Idle reads as a darkened small Silly Billy, sing poses become the spiked form, and hold notes keep the sing pose active.

Cutscene
Flashback during SO_STAY

The last ~16 seconds of the SO_STAY video are now swapped to a muted flashback cutscene (H.264 transcoded so it plays inside Chromium).

VFX
Speed lines + flashbang + power-50

After "Count Your Seconds!" a 10-second anime-style speed-lines burst plays. The mirror break triggers a 0.5s flashbang. The last 10 seconds drop into "power 50" drain.

Controls
Mobile + console support

Settings now has a Mobile toggle (4-lane on-screen buttons) and a Console toggle (Xbox / PlayStation / generic gamepads — D-pad and face buttons both map to the 4 lanes). Touch, keyboard, and gamepad all work simultaneously.

Builds
Online and offline rebuilt

Both Windows installers were refreshed with the Silly Billy assets, mobile/console support, and the new icon.

Quick Guide

Which one should friends use?

  • Best choice: send friends the hosted page and tell them to click Play Online In Browser.
  • Online: use this if friends are playing from different houses or different PCs.
  • Offline: use this if you just want local play with no server, or if two players are sharing one computer.
  • Mobile: open the browser version on a phone, then turn on Mobile in Settings — touch buttons appear for all 4 lanes.
  • Console: plug a controller in, turn on Console in Settings — D-pad and face buttons both fire the 4 lanes.
  • App: desktop apps are refreshed, but they are unsigned, so Windows may still show a warning.
  • JS: use this if you want the source files or want to self-host the build yourself.
